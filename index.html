Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 27, 2026 • Effective Date: October 27, 2026

🌿 Summary: 100% Local-First & Privacy by Design

PlantLog is designed from the ground up to respect your digital privacy. All your plant details, custom names, care logs, notes, and photos are stored 100% locally on your personal device. We do not require account registration, and your private plant photos and journals are never uploaded to any remote server or cloud storage.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how PlantLog ("we", "our", or "the App"), developed by ProgrammerCrow, handles information when you use our mobile application on iOS and Android devices.

We believe that caring for your houseplants should be a joyful, private, and uninterrupted experience. We are committed to transparency regarding our minimal data practices.

2. Data Collection & Storage Architecture

Unlike cloud-dependent services, PlantLog operates on an offline-first architecture:

  • No Account Required: You do not need to register with an email, phone number, social media account, or password.
  • Local Device Storage: Your houseplants' names, locations, watering schedules, care histories, and user notes are saved strictly on your device using private local storage (Hive and SharedPreferences).
  • Local Photos Sandbox: When you add or update photos of your plants, images are copied directly into the app’s secure local documents container. Photos never leave your device and are never transmitted to external cloud servers.

3. Device Permissions & How They Are Used

PlantLog requests only the minimal operating system permissions strictly necessary to provide its core features:

Permission Purpose & Usage Data Transfer
Camera Allows you to take live photos of your houseplants for visual growth tracking and avatar identification. 100% Local. Never transmitted externally.
Photo Library Allows you to select existing plant photos from your gallery. 100% Local. Never transmitted externally.
Notifications Allows the app to schedule local watering, fertilizing, misting, and care alarms at your preferred times. 100% Local OS alerts. No remote push server used.

4. Third-Party Services & Advertising (Google AdMob)

To keep PlantLog accessible and continuously updated with new botanical species and disease diagnosis guides, the app displays non-intrusive advertisements served by Google Mobile Ads (AdMob).

In compliance with Apple's App Store Nutrition Labels and Google Play Data Safety standards, Google AdMob may collect and process certain non-personally identifiable technical information:

  • Identifiers: Device advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, AAID on Android) or instance IDs used strictly for ad delivery and fraud prevention.
  • Usage & Interaction Data: Ad views, clicks, and basic user interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostics & Performance: Crash logs, app responsiveness metrics, and diagnostic technical data.

We do not sell your data to data brokers, and we do not perform cross-app tracking without explicit user consent via Apple's App Tracking Transparency framework where applicable.

For more information on how Google manages advertising data, please review the Google Privacy & Terms for Advertising.

5. Data Retention & Deletion Rights

Because all your personal plant data is stored locally on your device, you have complete and absolute control over your information at all times:

  • Deleting a Single Plant: Deleting a plant from "Ormanım" immediately and permanently removes all its care logs and associated photo files from your phone's storage.
  • Complete Data Erasure: Deleting or uninstalling the PlantLog application from your device instantly and permanently removes all databases, settings, and cached assets. No trace remains on any remote server.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

PlantLog is a safe, botanical hobby and houseplant care application suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or store personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe any child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the app, please contact us immediately.

7. Your Rights (GDPR & CCPA / CPRA)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

  • You have the right to access, rectify, or erase your data at any time by simply viewing, modifying, or deleting records within the app.
  • Because we do not store your identity, IP address, or user accounts on central servers, we hold no personally identifiable records that can be linked to your identity.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically to reflect app improvements or regulatory updates. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
